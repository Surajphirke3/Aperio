## 🌱 The ESG & Regulatory Tailwind

This is the most important macro trend supporting our business:

### India's Plastic Waste Regulations
- **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)** mandates — companies must prove they're using recycled plastic
- **BIS certification** for recycled plastic requires traceability documentation
- **CPCB compliance** requirements for recycling plants are tightening year by year

### Global Buyer Pressure
- Unilever, P&G, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo have all committed to 50%+ recycled content by 2030
- They need **verifiable supply chains** — our traceability data becomes their compliance proof
- Premium for certified recycled plastic: **40–60% above spot price**

### Carbon Markets
- India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) launched in 2023
- Recycling plants can earn carbon credits — but need verified data to claim them
- We provide the data infrastructure for carbon credit monetization

## 🚀 Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Hackathon to Pilot (Month 0–3)
- Win hackathon → Get press coverage + credibility
- Approach 5 recycling plants in Mumbai/Pune for free pilot
- Partner with industry associations (CIPET, AIPCMA)
- Build case studies showing 15–20% loss reduction

### Phase 2 — Early Revenue (Month 3–12)
- Convert pilots to paid (Growth plan)
- Partner with Recykal/other platforms as a plug-in module
- Attend Plastivision India, WasteExpo India for visibility
- Offer free compliance report → upsell to full platform

### Phase 3 — Scale (Year 2–3)
- WhatsApp integration for workers who don't use computers
- Hindi/Marathi language support
- Expand to Bangladesh, Vietnam, Indonesia (similar market dynamics)
- Carbon credit marketplace integration
